Good to know

Shopping made simple.

Quick answers before you place your order.

How do I order?

Open a product, review its details and use the WhatsApp inquiry button, or add it to your bag and send your order enquiry.

Do you deliver across Kenya?

Yes. We serve customers across Kenya. Delivery timing and fee can be confirmed with your order enquiry.

Can I pay with M-Pesa?

Yes. M-Pesa is supported for order enquiries and payment arrangements. Card payment can also be offered where available at checkout.

Can I ask about size or availability?

Yes. The WhatsApp button carries the product name, price, SKU, availability and product link so our team can help quickly.
